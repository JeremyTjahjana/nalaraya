# Nalaraya design
White #fff, ink #161616, muted #64645e, line #deded8, chemistry #bd2929. Atkinson Hyperlegible, readable line height; square controls. Landing is typography-led. Lab prioritizes canvas and measurement over decoration. Green and blue only identify future subjects.
## Direction contract
THESIS: a readable lab manual surrounding a manipulable experiment.
OWN-WORLD: white paper, black type, thin rules, red chemistry labels.
STORY: understand the purpose, select chemistry, rehearse a procedure, inspect the result.
FIRST VIEWPORT: large left-aligned title, concise description, quiet subject navigation.
FORM: user-pinned manual laboratory direction; code-first.
FINISH: inspect desktop/mobile, test scientific state, document remaining limitations.
